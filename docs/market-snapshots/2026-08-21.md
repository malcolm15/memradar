## What the numbers say

**The memory price crisis is visible as a step, not a slope.** DDR5 is +2.1% /
+4.9% / +2.5% across 1M/3M/6M but **+340.8% over a year**. The rise happened in
roughly Oct-Dec 2025 (individual PDP charts show kits going ~$105 → ~$430 in
that stretch); since then prices have **plateaued at the elevated level**. All
four segments corroborate the shape, with 1Y between +157% and +341%.

That is the guide's core claim in one table: buyers who missed the pre-crisis
window are not currently watching prices fall back, and "wait for a dip" advice
needs to reckon with a nine-month plateau rather than a spike.

**Secondary observations.** DDR4 is the only segment negative over 6M (-14.7%)
while still +183.8% over 1Y. NVMe's 3M figure (+19.7%) is the largest recent
move in any segment, and worth re-checking before publication in case it is
composition drift rather than a real move (n drops 78 → 63 between 6M and 3M).
