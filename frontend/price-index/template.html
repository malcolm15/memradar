Methodology

Every figure is a median, not an average. A single mispriced listing at $1,900 drags a mean badly in a segment of thirty products; the median reports the typical price and stays stable as the catalog changes.

Each period compares the same set of products in both timeframes. A product only counts toward a period if we have both a current price and a price from that period's baseline window, and both medians are computed over that same subset. Products added recently cannot distort a one-year figure by appearing on only one side of it. Subset sizes differ between periods for this reason, and that is the honest result rather than a flaw.
