MemRadar for Raycast

Search MemRadar's RAM and SSD prices without opening a browser. A free extension for Raycast on macOS, available in the Raycast Store.

Two commands: one to look up a product's price and history, one to see how each part of the market has moved.

Get it in the Raycast Store

Requires Raycast on macOS. Free and open source under the MIT license.

What it does

Search Memory Prices

Search the RAM and SSD products MemRadar tracks by name, brand or ASIN. Each result shows the current price and a marker for how it compares with that product's 90-day average. Press Enter for the product's price history: the all-time low and high with the dates they were set, the 90-day average, and one price point per month.

Show Market Overview

See how prices have moved in each segment we track: DDR5, DDR4, NVMe and SATA SSDs. For each one, the median price, the price per gigabyte, and the change over the past one, three, six and twelve months.

Shortcuts

  • Enter: show the product's price history
  • Cmd+Enter: open the product on memradar.com
  • Cmd+D: show or hide the side pane
  • Refresh: Raycast's standard refresh shortcut

About the data

The extension uses the same price history as the rest of MemRadar. Current prices are refreshed daily; price history is one point per month.

Price history is sourced from Keepa (keepa.com) under license and published here at one point per month with Keepa's written permission. Figures computed by MemRadar from that history. This data is provided for use with the MemRadar Raycast extension; it does not carry a right to redistribute the price history.

After the first load, the extension keeps a copy on your Mac, so search still works when you are offline.
